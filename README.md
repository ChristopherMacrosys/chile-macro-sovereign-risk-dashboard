# Chile Macroeconomic & Sovereign Risk Dashboard 

## Overview

This project presents an institutional-style sovereign risk dashboard analyzing Chile’s macro-financial vulnerabilities, sovereign spreads, external-sector sensitivity, FX dynamics, reserve accumulation, and medium-term sovereign outlook.

The dashboard integrates macroeconomic indicators, market-sensitive variables, and scenario-based sovereign interpretation to evaluate Chile’s resilience under changing global financial conditions and commodity-market dynamics.

---

## Core Analytical Themes

* Sovereign spread normalization and external repricing risk
* Current-account adjustment and external financing sensitivity
* Copper dependence and FX transmission dynamics
* Reserve accumulation and external liquidity resilience
* Fiscal flexibility and debt sustainability
* Structural productivity and competitiveness constraints
* Sovereign Vulnerability Index framework
* Baseline, upside, and downside sovereign-risk scenarios

---

## Key Sovereign-Risk Interpretation

Chile remains one of Latin America’s more institutionally credible and macro-financially resilient sovereigns. However, tighter global financial conditions, structurally weaker productivity growth, and continued sensitivity to external financing conditions continue to expose the sovereign to periods of externally transmitted volatility.

Copper remains a central stabilizing component within Chile’s sovereign framework due to its importance for export revenues, fiscal performance, and exchange-rate stability. Nevertheless, the economy remains vulnerable to commodity-price repricing, shifts in global liquidity conditions, and fluctuations in investor risk sentiment.
